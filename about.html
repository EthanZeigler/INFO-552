Who is Rebecca?

I am a Reader

Which shouldn't be a surprise for an asiring librarian.

I've always loved reading and stories, to the point that I used to hide a flashlight under my pillow so I could read at night. While the time I have to read ebbs and flows, my love for it has never waivered.

My Book Recommendations

  • Mr. Penumbra's 24 Hour Bookstore by Robin Sloan
  • Legends and Lattes by Travis Baldree
  • Shadow and Bone by Leigh Bardugo
  • Lessons in Chemistry by Bonnie Garmus
  • Jam by Yahtzee Croshaw

I am a Chef

Not professionally, of course!

I love cooking and pushing myself to try new recipes. In fact, I meet with my mom monthly just to try new recipes and learn from "the master," and every other month, my best friend and I will spend the night cooking!

This hobby also gives me a great excuse to go to the farmer's market every weekend!

My Top Five Favorite Recipes

I am a Gamer

I didn't think I was a gamer, but that all changed semi-recently when I realized just how many games I love.

I've always loved interesting card games. Family game night almost exclusively involved card games like Flux, Gulliotine, and the most wholesome family game, Cards Against Humanity.

I would also primarily play board games with my brother or my friends, like Scene It, Monopoly, and Risk. However, I started breaking into more stategic-based board games in adulthood, like Harvest and Flamecraft.

Finally, I really enjoy video games. I played the Nintendo Wii as a child (Mario Kart Wii was my game growing up), but I became more invested in games I could play on my laptop, such as Undertale, the Sims 4, and my absolute favorite game of all-time, Stardew Valley.

My Favorite Games (and the devices I play on)

  • Stardew Valley (PC)
  • Harvest (Board Game)
  • Gulliotine (Card Game)
  • Date Everything (Switch)

I am a Dungeon Master

In addition to playing games with definitive rules and set-ups, I also play Dungeons and Dragons with my friends. Currently, I'm running a homebrew campaign where all the players are entrepreneurs trying to start their businesses in a fantasy world setting (inspired by Legends and Lattes by Travis Baldree).

I am a Cartoon Enthusiast

I was raised on animation. My father has a particular love of Looney Tunes, which was expressed through cell animation decorating the house and a plethora of blue button-down Looney Tunes work shirts. My baby wallpaper was also Baby Looney Tunes, so I was started on this passion when I was very young.

As I got older, I grew an appreciation for all aspects of the animation process. I particularly love voice actors, with my all-time favorite being Rob Paulsen (best known for his work in Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles and Animaniacs.

My Favorite Cartoons

  • Looney Tunes
  • Gravity Falls
  • Ducktales (2017)
  • Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012)

I am a World Traveler

I got my first passport when I was ten, and I've used it extensively.

I've now visited over a dozen countries, including a month-long study abroad trip to Chengdu, China, and a six-month long study abroad trip to Wroxton, England.

Despite the state of the world, I have no intention of stopping my travels. In fact, I hope to visit Japan and Spain in the next decade!

Meet the Cat-cuterie

Honey & Brioche

I adopted Autumn "Honey" in October of 2022 after moving into my new apartment, and she immediately became my shadow!

She loves to chat on the phone, help out with the plants, and cuddle on the couch. But I was always worried that she was lonely when I went to work.

In January, I adopted Clover "Brioche" (also referred to as "Brie") after meeting her at a cat adoption bookstore event. She loves doing parkour off my bookshelves, screaming at the neighbors, and purring.

After surviving the month-long separation period, these two are now as close as sisters!
