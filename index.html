My Name is Rebecca Chasen

Aspiring Librarian ~ Third Year Drexel Student

"All the secrets of the world worth knowing are hiding in plain sight."

-Robin Sloan, Mr. Penumbra's 24 Hour Bookstore

For as long as I can remember, I have loved stories. I would stay up late reading, my allowance was spent at the bookstore, and the library is my haven. When I pursued my BA/MA in communications, I was drawn to the idea that everything was related in some way to storytelling, from relaying simple messages to my colleagues to researching ways to convey a brand’s story to an entire varied audience. Mostly, I loved working collaboratively with people to reach the goals they had to complete the story they needed to tell.

After entering the workforce, I realized that the aspects of my work that I connected with the most occurred when I was helping others in their searches, such as assisting a client to find what they needed or doing research to support my coworkers with their projects. I realized that I am more interested in collaborative experiences than working directly on marketing and campaigns, which is much of what my communications degree focused on. For this reason, I have realized that I want to pursue a different career where I could still utilize the skills and knowledge I have already gained while working more directly with others and their goals.

My path became clear after talking with Jane Greenberg. She showed me how library sciences includes many of my professional interests. It works with data in many forms, and its a field in service to the community directly. My resolve was strengthened by speaking with other professionals in the library and information ﬁeld. At this time, I plan to pursue my MIS in Library Science at Drexel this fall so I can broaden my knowledge and skills to become a well- rounded professional and enter a career in librarianship.

My short-term career plan is to earn my MS in Library Science while working in a job more suited to customer service, as this would help keep my communication and service skills sharp as I broaden my knowledge with my studies. In the long-term, I would like to transition my career into librarianship. I want to help people ﬁnd the information they need through my passion for learning and research.
