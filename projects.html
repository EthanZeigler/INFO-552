Library of Congress Capstone

For my Capstone, I worked with the Law Library of Congress on their Congressional Bill Summaries project. Their goal was to convert the PDF scans of the original historical bill summaries from 1930-1972 into text files, making them more accessible, discoverable, and readable. My job was to edit the metadata spreadsheets and summary text files to better reflect their content.

The metadata that I created, reviewed, and edited was submitted to the Library’s Congressional Research Service (or CRS), who will make it widely available on the Library of Congress’s website.

INFO 662 Metadata Profile

For this assignment, I was asked to complete five to eight full metadata records with at least 2 standards, however I love a challenge, and I really wanted to learn how to improve my metadata recording skills. So I decided to create eight metadata records with five different standards.

I initally wanted to create each record on a single focus object, but I was highly advised to rething my plan so I could practice more with each item. However, I still decided to focus primarily on books since it is one of my favorite things!
